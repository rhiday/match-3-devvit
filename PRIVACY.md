# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 29, 2026

## Overview

SnapMatch is a match-3 puzzle game built on Reddit's Devvit platform. We take your privacy seriously and believe in transparency about data collection and use.

## What Data We Collect

### Game Data
- **Reddit Username**: Used to display your identity on leaderboards and track your personal scores
- **Reddit User ID**: Used internally to associate scores with your account
- **Game Scores**: Your scores and statistics for each daily puzzle
- **Gameplay Metrics**: Number of chains, matches, and other game performance data

### Technical Data
- Data is stored via Reddit's Devvit platform infrastructure
- We do not collect IP addresses, device information, or tracking cookies
- We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services

## How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:
- Displaying your username and scores on in-game leaderboards
- Tracking your personal best scores for each puzzle
- Enabling fair competition through asynchronous multiplayer gameplay
- Improving game balance and features

## Data Storage & Retention

- All data is stored on Reddit's secure infrastructure via the Devvit platform
- Leaderboard data persists indefinitely to maintain competitive integrity
- Personal best scores are retained to track your progress over time

## Data Sharing

- Your username and scores are **publicly visible** on in-game leaderboards to all players
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties
- We do **not** share data with advertisers or analytics companies

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Stop playing at any time (scores already posted will remain on leaderboards)
- Request deletion of your personal data by contacting us (see below)
- View your own scores and statistics in-game

## Children's Privacy

SnapMatch is intended for users 13 years and older, in accordance with Reddit's Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the game constitutes acceptance of any changes.
